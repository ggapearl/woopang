> Effective October 24, 2026

QUE.ENT ("we") processes the personal information of people who use the WOOPANG app and woopang.com (the "Service") in accordance with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. This policy explains what we collect and why, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it or stop its use.

## 1. What we collect and why
### Sign-up and login
- Email sign-up: user ID, email address, password (stored with one-way encryption)
- Kakao, Google or Apple sign-up: user ID, plus the email address and account identifier that service provides
- Country code derived from your IP address at sign-up — looked up with data kept on our own server; your IP address is not sent anywhere
- Used for: identifying members, login, preventing abuse
### Profile (only if you add it)
- Profile photo, bio, mobile number, Instagram, Facebook and X handles
- Used for: your profile as other users see it. Your email address and mobile number are not shown to other users
### Location
- Your current location while you use the app: to show nearby places, AR objects, the map and the weather, and to pick local information and ads
- Nearby people: while location sharing is on, other users can see your location according to your visibility and sharing-radius settings
- Treasure hunts: distance guidance for games you join, confirming a find, and alerts about new games nearby
- Notifications: for devices that allow location use, we keep the last location and its approximate address to send nearby news
- We do not build a history of where you have been. Each feature keeps only your last location and overwrites it with the new one
- The locations of places, speech bubbles and treasures you post are stored with that content
### Content and activity
- Places (name, location, photos, description), comments, likes, speech bubbles, reports
- Treasure hunt games, participation, finds and reviews
- Direct messages (sender, recipient, content, read status), follow and block lists
- Used for: providing the Service, handling reports, preventing abuse
### Device and notifications
- Push token, device identifier, device name and model, OS and app version, last active time
- Used for: sending notifications, keeping per-device settings
### Collected automatically
- Server access logs (IP address, time, requested address, including coordinates when nearby information is requested): security and troubleshooting
- App error reports (error message, app version, device model, OS, screen name): fixing errors. They are not linked to your account
### Camera and photos
- The camera view is processed only on your phone to display AR and is never sent to our servers
- Photos are sent to our servers only when you choose or take them to post

## 2. How long we keep it
- Account information and content: until you delete your account. When you do, we delete without delay everything you posted, including places, comments, messages, treasure hunt records, speech bubbles and reports
- Location: only the last location per feature, overwritten by the new one, and deleted when you delete your account
- Server backups: backups kept for recovery are deleted in turn after 14 days
- Access logs: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act) — split by day, with each day's log deleted once it is 3 months old

## 3. Sharing with others
- We do not provide your personal information to other companies or institutions, except when investigative or other authorities request it through procedures set by law
- By the nature of the Service, other users can see your user ID and profile, the places, comments, speech bubbles and treasure hunts you post, and your location while location sharing is on

## 4. Service providers and transfers abroad
Your personal information is stored on servers we operate ourselves in Korea. We entrust only the delivery of notifications to the companies below, and send the data over the network each time a notification is delivered.
- Android notifications — Google LLC (USA, support.google.com/policies), Firebase Cloud Messaging: push token and notification content
- iPhone notifications — Apple Inc. (USA, apple.com/legal/privacy/contact), Apple Push Notification service: push token and notification content
- Retention: until the notification is delivered (up to 4 weeks if your phone is off)
- If you do not want this, turn off WOOPANG notifications in your phone settings. Nothing is sent to these companies, and you will not receive notifications
### Lookups that send coordinates only
These lookups send coordinates only, without anything that identifies you such as your name, user ID or contact details. Our server makes the request on your behalf, so your IP address is not passed on either.
- Weather — OpenMeteo GmbH (Switzerland), Open-Meteo: coordinates rounded to about 5 km
- Addresses and neighborhood names — VWorld (Korean Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport), Kakao Local, OpenStreetMap Foundation (UK) Nominatim
- Nearby public places and transit — Korea Public Data Portal (Korea Tourism Organization, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport)
- When the app is not in Korean or you are outside Korea, the app sends coordinates directly to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (UK) Nominatim to get an address. In that case your phone's IP address also reaches that server
### Signing in with another company
- If you choose Kakao, Google or Apple login, you sign in on that company's screen and we receive only the result (account identifier and email address)
### Features that hand off to another app, only when you tap them
- Ask AI: hands a question made from your neighborhood name and the nearby place list to the AI app you choose (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini). Coordinates are not included
- Translate screen: saves the current screen to your photo library and opens the Google Translate web page. You decide whether to upload the photo

## 5. Your rights
- You can edit your profile or delete your account in Edit Profile
- In Settings you can turn off nearby location sharing and treasure hunt participation and alerts
- You can turn off location, notification, camera and photo permissions in your phone settings at any time. Features that need them will stop working
- For other requests to access, correct, delete or stop processing your information, email master@woopang.com and we will act within 10 days. You may also make a request through a representative

## 6. How we delete information
- We delete information without delay when its retention period ends or when you delete your account
- Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered

## 7. How we protect it
- Communication between the app and our servers, and between our servers and outside services, is encrypted (HTTPS)
- Passwords are stored with one-way encryption
- Our database cannot be reached from outside, and access to admin pages is restricted
- We keep the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum

## 8. Cookies and tracking
- The app does not use cookies. Only the web login and sign-up pages use a session cookie while you log in
- We do not use ad tracking or behavioral analytics tools. Ads in the app are placed by us directly, and we count only how often they are shown and tapped

## 9. Children under 14
- WOOPANG is not intended for children under 14. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 14, we delete it without delay
